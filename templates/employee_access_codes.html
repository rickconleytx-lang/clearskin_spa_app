Secure Session Timeout

After an employee enters their Employee Verification Code, their verified secure session remains active while they work within protected areas of Peach Suite Pro. They may move between protected pages without entering their code again.

If they leave a protected area, a 15-second grace period begins. Returning to a protected page within 15 seconds keeps the secure session active. After 15 seconds outside the protected area, the secure session ends and the employee must enter their Employee Verification Code again.

Selecting End Current Session or pressing Esc ends the secure session immediately.
