SMS Compliance

SMS platform access, consent, and compliance requirements

SMS Platform Access and Future Business User Requirements

Peach Suite Pro is committed to maintaining the highest standards of SMS compliance and consumer protection. SMS messaging capabilities are disabled by default for all future business users and cannot be activated until the business has successfully completed all required compliance verification.

Prior to enabling SMS functionality, each business must provide documentation demonstrating compliance with applicable carrier and regulatory requirements, including, but not limited to, evidence of an approved U.S. A2P 10DLC campaign (or other applicable messaging registration), copies of its SMS Consent Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, documented opt-in procedures, sample message content, and any additional supporting documentation requested by Just Peachy Data.

Each participating business is solely responsible for obtaining and maintaining valid consumer consent before transmitting SMS messages through Peach Suite Pro. Just Peachy Data reserves the right to deny, suspend, or revoke SMS access for any business that fails to satisfy these requirements or that violates applicable laws, carrier policies, industry standards, or platform communication policies.

No business user may transmit SMS messages through Peach Suite Pro until all required documentation has been reviewed and approved by Just Peachy Data and SMS functionality has been expressly activated for that account.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS TO US!

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do NOT sell your data.

SMS OPT - OUT

Just Peachy Data and Peach Suite Pro

ALL SMS messages transmitted through the platform contain a hard-coded compliance Opt-Out notice.

This compliance notice is automatically inserted at the time of SMS transmission and cannot be altered, modified, or removed by users of the platform.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:

STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT.

After opting out of SMS messages, you may receive one final confirmation message confirming your opt-out request. You will no longer receive SMS communications unless you provide new consent.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact support.
