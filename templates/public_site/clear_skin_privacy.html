Information we collect

Clear Skin Esthetics may collect information that clients provide when using the website or booking services, including names, telephone numbers, email addresses, appointment details, service selections, communication preferences, notes submitted by the client, and records of interactions with the business.

The website and its service providers may also collect technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Schedule, manage, confirm, and provide appointments;
  • Send requested service communications and reminders;
  • Respond to questions and provide client support;
  • Maintain business, accounting, security, and compliance records;
  • Improve services, website performance, and client experience;
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized access; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Payments

Payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. Clear Skin Esthetics and Peach Suite Pro should not receive or store a complete payment-card number unless a specific payment feature clearly states otherwise.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be shared with vendors that help operate the business and provide booking, hosting, email, text messaging, payment, security, analytics, or support services. These providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to perform their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect clients or the business, to investigate misuse, or as part of a lawful business transfer.

SMS privacy

Clear Skin Esthetics does not sell or share mobile opt-in information or SMS consent with unrelated third parties for their own promotional or marketing purposes.

Mobile information and SMS consent status may be shared with Peach Suite Pro, telecommunications providers, and other vendors that help deliver and manage the messaging program.

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain business and appointment records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, security, or compliance obligations.

Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No website, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children and minors

This website is not directed to children under 13. Services for minors may require the involvement or consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Your choices

Clients may update certain information by contacting Clear Skin Esthetics. SMS recipients may opt out by replying STOP. Marketing email recipients may use the unsubscribe method included in the message.

Contact

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to Clear Skin Esthetics at (817) 674-7025.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The effective date at the top of the page identifies the current version.
