Appointments

Appointments are subject to availability and are not guaranteed until the booking process is completed and a confirmation is issued. Please review the selected service, provider, date, and time carefully.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Clear Skin Esthetics does not currently charge a cancellation or no-show fee.

Clients are asked to cancel or reschedule as soon as reasonably possible so the appointment time may be offered to another client. Cancellations and rescheduling requests may be made through the available booking tools or by contacting Clear Skin Esthetics at (817) 674-7025.

Repeated missed appointments or last-minute cancellations may result in future appointments requiring manual approval or a deposit disclosed before booking. No such deposit will be charged without advance notice.

Late arrivals

Please arrive on time. A late arrival may require the service to be shortened, modified, or rescheduled so later clients are not delayed. The full scheduled service may not be possible when a client arrives late.

Provider changes and business cancellations

Clear Skin Esthetics may need to reschedule or cancel an appointment because of illness, emergencies, weather, equipment issues, or other circumstances. When practical, reasonable efforts will be made to contact the client and offer another available appointment.

Service suitability and health information

Clients should provide complete and accurate information about allergies, sensitivities, medications, recent procedures, pregnancy, and other conditions that may affect a service. Clear Skin Esthetics may decline, postpone, or modify a treatment when doing so is reasonably necessary for safety.

Prices, promotions, and gift certificates

Prices, service descriptions, availability, promotions, packages, and gift-certificate terms may change. Any material price or deposit requirement will be shown or communicated before the appointment is confirmed.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version posted on this website at the time of booking will apply unless Clear Skin Esthetics provides different written terms.
