Program name and consent method

The Clear Skin Esthetics service-messaging program provides appointment-related text messages to clients who voluntarily opt in through an unchecked checkbox in the online booking form.

Checking the box is optional. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing or booking a service.

Types of messages

Service-related messages may include:

  • Appointment confirmations;
  • Appointment reminders;
  • Scheduling or rescheduling updates;
  • Cancellation notices;
  • Provider or location updates; and
  • Replies to client-initiated appointment questions.

Marketing messages

The service-message checkbox does not authorize promotional or marketing text messages. Marketing texts, when offered, require a separate, optional, unchecked consent checkbox.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies according to appointments and client requests. Message and data rates may apply under the recipient's wireless plan. Wireless carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to opt out. Clear Skin Esthetics will stop further messages covered by the opt-out request, except for a final confirmation or messages otherwise permitted by law.

A client may also communicate a clear request to stop text messages through another reasonable contact method. After opting out, appointment communications may be provided by email or telephone when available.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact Clear Skin Esthetics at (817) 674-7025.

Privacy

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are not sold or shared with unrelated third parties for their own marketing. Information may be shared with Peach Suite Pro, telecommunications providers, and other vendors that help deliver and administer the messaging service. See the Privacy Policy for additional information.

Changes to the program

Clear Skin Esthetics may modify or discontinue the messaging program. Material changes will be reflected in the version of these SMS Terms posted on this website.
