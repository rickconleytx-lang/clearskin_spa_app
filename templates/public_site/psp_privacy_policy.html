Privacy Policy

Peach Suite Pro by Just Peachy Data LLC

Effective Date: April 12, 2026

Introduction

Peach Suite Pro is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business management platform developed and owned by Just Peachy Data. This Privacy Policy explains how Peach Suite Pro collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by its users.

Information We Collect

Peach Suite Pro may collect:

  • Account registration information
  • Business information
  • Customer and client contact information
  • Appointment and scheduling information
  • Health and intake information entered by users
  • Payment-related transaction information
  • SMS and email communication preferences
  • Device, browser, IP address, and usage information
  • Support requests and feedback

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Peach Suite Pro platform
  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Deliver account notifications and customer care communications
  • Improve platform performance and security
  • Provide technical support
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

SMS and Email Communications

Users who provide express consent may receive SMS or email communications including account notifications, customer care messages, appointment reminders, and service-related updates.

Message frequency may vary. Standard message and data rates may apply. Users may opt out of SMS by replying STOP and may request assistance by replying HELP.

Marketing communications, if offered, will be subject to separate consent where required by law.

Information Sharing

Peach Suite Pro does not sell customer or mobile information.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes.

Information may be shared only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the platform, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the service.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards are implemented to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and ensuring that information entered into the platform is accurate.

Data Retention

Information is retained as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Third-Party Services

Peach Suite Pro may integrate with third-party providers including messaging services, payment processors, email providers, and cloud infrastructure providers. Those providers maintain their own privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Peach Suite Pro is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any revisions.
