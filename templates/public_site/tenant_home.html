Text Messaging

SMS Messaging & Consent

{{ business_name }} offers optional text messaging to clients who expressly choose to participate.

How to Opt In

Clients may opt in during online booking by selecting the applicable optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox.

Service messages may include appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling or rescheduling updates, cancellation notices, provider or location updates, and replies to appointment-related questions.

{% if website_settings .include_marketing_sms_in_10dlc_application %}

Marketing messages may include special offers, service information, business updates, and event announcements. Marketing consent is requested through a separate optional, unchecked checkbox and is not combined with service-message consent.

{% endif %}

How to Opt Out or Get Help

Reply STOP to opt out of text messages. A final confirmation message may be sent after an opt-out request.

Reply HELP for assistance. Clients may also contact {{ business_name }} to withdraw consent through another reasonable method.

After opting out, appointment communications may be provided by email or telephone when available.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent information will not be shared with any third parties.
